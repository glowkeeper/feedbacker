A new chapter

Assessment needs more than an answer.

Feedbacker is becoming a workflow, governance, and assessment layer around AI—helping educators work responsibly, consistently, and at scale.

The project is being redefined and rebuilt in the open.

What endures

AI assists.
Educators decide.

01

Human authority

Marks and released feedback remain the responsibility of educators—not an automated system.

02

Visible provenance

Inputs, criteria, transformations, suggestions, edits, and approvals should be traceable.

03

Institutional control

Models, prompts, data handling, retention, and deployment should be governable and replaceable.

04

Cohort consistency

Rubric-led workflows should support moderation and calibration without flattening academic judgement.

The direction

Responsible assessment infrastructure, built with educators.

The previous application has been retired. Its successor starts with governance, privacy, accessibility, and the realities of academic practice—not with a model or a prompt.
